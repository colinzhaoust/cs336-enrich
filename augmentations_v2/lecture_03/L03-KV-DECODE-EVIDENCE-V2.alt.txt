Autoregressive decode adds one token at a time and repeatedly reads a growing K/V cache. In a declared 8-query-head, 128-head-dimension BF16 model with 32 layers and 4096 tokens, MHA stores 4 KiB per cached token per layer and 512 MiB model-wide. Keeping eight query heads but changing K/V heads from eight to one gives the MQA capacity extreme at 64 MiB; using two K/V groups gives a GQA point at 128 MiB.
