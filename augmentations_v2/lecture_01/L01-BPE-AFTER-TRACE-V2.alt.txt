An ordered three-rule BPE merge table is learned on “the cat in the hat” and then frozen. On held-out “the quick brown fox,” the encoder replays t+h→256, 256+e→257, and 257+space→258, reducing 19 byte tokens to 16 without recomputing pair counts or changing the vocabulary. The final ledger contrasts training, where counts, vocabulary, rules, and sequence change, with encoding, where only the held-out sequence changes.
