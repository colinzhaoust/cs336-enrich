How to leave useful feedback

Every concept has a stable segment ID. Use it to connect a learner's confusion to the exact source, storyboard, animation, and revision.

  1. 1Read the source

    Start from the left column and note the source line or slide.

  2. 2Inspect the augmentation

    Play, scrub, or step through the explanation on the right.

  3. 3Discuss the segment

    Say what you expected, where you lost the thread, and what visual change might help.

  4. 4Follow the revision

    The maintainer replies with the updated page and commit after a change lands.
