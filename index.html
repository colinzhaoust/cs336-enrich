A source-aligned companion to Stanford CS336

The companion follows each lecture in order and places transcript-indexed excerpts, official slide or code locations, formula comparisons, resource-accounting tables, interactive models, slow visual replays, evidence summaries, and further reading at the moment they are useful.

Scope and attribution. The original lectures remain the primary course material. Professor content, our source-faithful paraphrases, finished augmentations, and planned work are labeled separately. This project is not affiliated with Stanford University.
